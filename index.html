About us

DRDO is the research arm of the Ministry of Defence. We develop cutting-edge weapon systems, sensors and platforms, and transfer technology to industry so the country can produce them at home.

Vision

Empower India with cutting-edge defence technologies and a strong science and industry base.

Mission

Design, develop and deliver state-of-the-art sensors, weapon systems and platforms for the armed forces.

Industry partnership

Transfer of technology to public and private firms, startups and MSMEs across the country.

Skilled workforce

Scientists, engineers and technicians working across a national network of laboratories.
